## Platform

## Product Purpose

Bing-Yang Chen's bilingual personal website helps recruiters, technical peers, and potential collaborators understand his background, work, and interests. See the [site design context](docs/design.md) for the editorial and visual direction.

## Product Principles

- Use the homepage to introduce the author and his work. Use the resume to document specific career accomplishments.
- Give visitors clear paths to inspect projects, read articles, or make contact.
- Keep personal and professional claims grounded in the author's evidence.

## Evidence on Hand

Use the author’s [original photographs](src/assets/about/photography/) and the [shared career and profile facts](docs/architecture.md#where-to-edit-content) when exploring new designs.
